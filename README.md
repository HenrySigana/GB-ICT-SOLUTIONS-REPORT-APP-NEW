# GB ICT Solutions Sales Tracker v2

## Overview

GB ICT Solutions Sales Tracker v2 is a comprehensive business management system designed for cyber cafés, ICT service providers, M-Pesa agents, retail shops, and small businesses. The platform combines sales tracking, expense management, inventory control, customer relationship management, invoicing, reporting, and business analytics into a single web-based application.

The system is built as a Progressive Web Application (PWA), allowing installation on desktops, laptops, tablets, and mobile devices for a seamless user experience.

---

## Key Features

### Sales Management

* Record and manage sales transactions.
* Support multiple payment methods:

  * Cash
  * M-Pesa
  * Bank Transfer
  * Credit/Mkopo
* Generate customer receipts instantly.

### Expense Management

* Record daily business expenses.
* Categorize expenses for accurate reporting.
* Monitor operating costs and profitability.

### Inventory Management

* Track stock levels in real time.
* Receive low-stock alerts.
* Monitor inventory costs and stock movement.

### Customer Loyalty Management

* Store customer information.
* Track customer visits and spending.
* Manage loyalty rewards and customer engagement.

### Reporting & Analytics

* Daily sales reports.
* Monthly business reports.
* Profit and Loss statements.
* Revenue and expense analysis.
* Business performance insights.

### M-Pesa Agent Operations

* Record deposits and withdrawals.
* Track agent commissions.
* Monitor float balances.
* Generate transaction summaries.

### Receipt & Invoice Management

* Generate printable receipts.
* Create professional invoices.
* Export reports and documents to PDF and Excel.

### AI Business Insights

* Analyze business performance.
* Generate recommendations and observations.
* Monitor trends and profitability.

### Backup & Recovery

* Export business data for backup.
* Restore data when necessary.
* Protect critical business information.

## Installation

### Option 1: Web Access

1. Open the application URL in a supported browser.
2. Log in using your assigned PIN.
3. Begin managing your business operations.

### Option 2: Install as a PWA

1. Open the application in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the Install App icon in the browser.
3. Follow the installation prompts.
4. Launch the application from your desktop or device menu.

## Troubleshooting

### Unable to Log In

* Verify the correct PIN.
* Check internet connectivity.
* Contact the system administrator.

### Reports Not Generating

* Confirm transaction data exists.
* Refresh the application.
* Retry report generation.

### Sync Issues

* Check internet connection.
* Reload the application.
* Verify Supabase connectivity.
